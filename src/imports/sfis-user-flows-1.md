3️⃣ RECEIVE MONEY (ALLOWANCES)

Screen Title:
Receive Allowances

Description:
Your SFIS FinEra INCLUSIVE CREDIT Account allows you to receive student allowances from parents or sponsors through multiple payment channels.

Supported Channels

Remittance Services
• Mukuru
• WorldRemit
• International Money Transfer Partners

Mobile Money Platforms
• EcoCash
• InnBucks

How Senders Transfer Money

Sender selects SFIS Transfer on the remittance or mobile money platform.

Sender enters the student's SFIS Account Number or Registered Phone Number.

Funds are securely transferred to the student’s Savings Balance Wallet.

Note:
Funds received can be saved, used for approved campus expenses, or transferred within the SFIS ecosystem.

4️⃣ DASHBOARD CHANGE – CREDIT LIMIT

Replace:
Available Credit

With:
Credit Limit

Definition (Display Tooltip):

Credit Limit
The maximum amount of credit you are eligible to access within the SFIS FinEra INCLUSIVE CREDIT system. Your credit limit is determined by your Financial Discipline Score, savings behavior, and institutional eligibility.

6️⃣ SAVINGS BALANCE WALLET

Wallet Name:
Savings Balance

Description:
Your Savings Balance stores funds received from allowances, remittances, or transfers within the SFIS ecosystem.

You Can:

• Save received allowances
• Transfer money to other SFIS users
• Build savings to increase your Credit Limit
• Demonstrate financial discipline within the system

Registration → Account Number Generated → Phone Number Linked → Allowances Received → Savings Balance Updated → Credit Limit Determined → Internal Transfers Enabled

This ensures:

• Secure identification through phone number
• Easy allowance transfers from parents
• Financial discipline tracking through savings
• Controlled ecosystem transactions
